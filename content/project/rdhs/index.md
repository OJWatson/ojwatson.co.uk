rdhs brings survey discovery, data access and preparation into a reproducible R workflow. It is part of rOpenSci and has a dedicated software paper by Watson, FitzJohn and Eaton in *Wellcome Open Research* (2019). Access to individual survey datasets remains subject to DHS permissions.
