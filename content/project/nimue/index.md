nimue extends [squire](/project/squire/) to study vaccination. It was used in the analysis of the first year of COVID-19 vaccination by Watson and colleagues, published in *The Lancet Infectious Diseases* (2022).

That study has a [separate repository for its data and analysis](https://github.com/mrc-ide/covid-vaccine-impact-orderly). The modelling software and the study-specific analysis are complementary resources. The software repository does not provide a support commitment.
