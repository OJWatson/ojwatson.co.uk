outbreakteachR accompanies a practical in which students analyse a simulated classroom paper outbreak. The package supports teaching through data summaries and visualisation.

The associated paper by Cremin, Watson and colleagues appeared in *Epidemics*, volume 23 (2018; first published online in 2017). The repository includes the teaching materials and tutorial.
