magenta underpins a study evaluating genetic indicators of changes in malaria transmission. The project provides an R interface and documentation for the research software.

The associated study by Watson and colleagues appeared in *Molecular Biology and Evolution*, volume 38 (2021; first published online in 2020). The repository describes ongoing development rather than a supported service.
