roiv organises analyses of vaccine costs and benefits using an R package structure.

Related work includes [Hallie Benjamin and colleagues’ 2025 preprint on the global return on investment from the first year of COVID-19 vaccination](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.09.02.25334932). OJ Watson and Mark Jit are joint senior authors. This work is currently a preprint. Consult the manuscript and repository for the available data and analyses.
