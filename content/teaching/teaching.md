I teach across infectious disease modelling, health emergencies, and applied public health decision-making. My teaching is mostly at module level, with practical work kept focused on reproducible analysis and policy-relevant interpretation.

## Current teaching roles at Imperial

- **Global Master of Public Health (Health Protection module)**: Module co-lead since 2024/2025.
- **MSc Epidemiology (Outbreaks module)**: Module co-lead from 2025/2026, covering outbreak analysis and response in health emergencies.
- **MSc Epidemiology (Emerging and Neglected Tropical Diseases)**: Lecturer on planning and preparing outbreak research.

- **Ishana Atkinson** (PhD, UCL) — modelling and mapping vaccine equity, supported by OJ.

## External teaching and mentorship

- Contributions to Imperial's [**Mathematical Modelling for the Control of Infectious Diseases** short course](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/lifelong-learning/courses/mathematical-modelling-infectious-diseases/).
